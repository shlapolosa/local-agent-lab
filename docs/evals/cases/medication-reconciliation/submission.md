# Use case: medication reconciliation at discharge

## Problem
Patients leave the hospital with a discharge medication list that disagrees with what they were taking at home and with what the ward prescribed. Pharmacists reconcile by hand from three sources and the step is skipped under pressure, so avoidable adverse drug events and readmissions follow.

## For whom
Ward pharmacists, discharging physicians, the patient and their community pharmacy.

## What the solution does
1. Gathers the pre-admission medication history from the community pharmacy record and the patient's own account.
2. Compares it line by line with the inpatient prescriptions and the draft discharge list.
3. Flags omissions, duplications, dose changes and interactions, with a reason for each.
4. Drafts the reconciled discharge medication list for the pharmacist to confirm.
5. Produces the patient-facing medication schedule and sends the confirmed list to the community pharmacy and the GP.
6. Records who confirmed what, so the reconciliation is auditable.

## Data
Inpatient prescribing records, dispensing history, allergy list, the discharge summary draft, drug interaction reference.

## Constraints
A pharmacist confirms every change; nothing reaches the patient or the community pharmacy unconfirmed. Arabic and English patient schedules.
