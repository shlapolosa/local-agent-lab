# Use case: answering a staff policy question

## Problem

Staff ask HR and line managers the same policy questions repeatedly — leave carry-over, notice
periods, expense limits, what happens to unused study leave. The answers are all in the published
staff handbook and the HR policy set, but nobody reads forty pages to find one clause, so they ask a
person instead. HR estimates a third of its inbound queries are answerable from published policy.

## What we want

An assistant a member of staff can ask in plain language. It finds the relevant clause, answers from
it, and cites the clause so the person can check it. Where the policy does not cover the question, it
says so and hands the question to HR rather than guessing — a confidently wrong answer about notice
periods is worse than no answer.

## Scope

Published policy only. It does not read anyone's employment record, does not answer questions about
an individual's own entitlement, and changes nothing. One turn in, one answer out.
