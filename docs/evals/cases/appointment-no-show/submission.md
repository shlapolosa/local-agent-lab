# Use case: reducing outpatient no-shows

## Problem
One in five outpatient appointments is missed. Slots go unused while other patients wait weeks, reminders are one-size-fits-all, and the clinic cannot tell who is likely to miss until the day.

## For whom
Outpatient clinic schedulers and receptionists, clinic managers, and the patients on the waiting list.

## What the solution does
1. Estimates for each booked appointment how likely it is to be missed, from booking lead time, prior attendance, distance, and the appointment type.
2. Chooses a reminder plan for each patient — timing, channel and language — and sends it.
3. Offers a patient who signals they cannot attend an alternative slot, and releases the original.
4. Fills a released or predicted-empty slot from the waiting list in order of clinical priority.
5. Reports attendance, fill rates and the reminders' effect to the clinic manager each week.

## Data
Appointment book, attendance history, patient contact preferences, waiting-list priorities.

## Constraints
No slot is released without the patient's confirmation. Patient contact follows the consent recorded on the record.
