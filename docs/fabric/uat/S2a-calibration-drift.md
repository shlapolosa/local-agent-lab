# Device calibration drift — supplier obligations

Our diagnostic devices are maintained under contract by their suppliers. Each supplier provides a
supplier attestation at the point of installation, certifying the device against its reference
standard.

Calibration drift is the gradual divergence of a device's readings from that reference standard
between scheduled services. Our contracts do not currently define an acceptable calibration drift
threshold, nor do they say who bears the cost of an out-of-tolerance finding.

We propose that every supplier attestation state a calibration drift tolerance, and that breach of
that tolerance be a contractual trigger rather than a goodwill negotiation.
