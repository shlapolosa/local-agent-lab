# Supplier attestation — proposed process

A supplier attestation is the supplier's signed statement that a device meets its reference standard
on the day of installation or service.

Today attestations arrive by email and are filed by whoever receives them. We propose a register, so
that an attestation can be produced on demand and so that calibration drift findings can be matched
to the attestation they contradict.

The register would hold the attesting supplier, the device, the date, and the calibration drift
tolerance the attestation claims.
