# Weekly supplier-invoice exception list

## The problem
The accounts payable team receives around 90 supplier invoices a week. Most match a purchase order
and clear automatically. Roughly a dozen do not — a quantity differs, a line is missing a PO
reference, or the supplier name on the invoice differs from the one on the order.

Finding those dozen currently means one person opening the week's invoice register and comparing it
to the PO register by eye, every Monday morning. It takes about two hours, and the exceptions found
are the same handful of patterns each week.

## Who has it
One accounts payable clerk does the comparison. The Financial Controller sees the consequence: an
exception found on Monday rather than at month end is one that can still be fixed before the
payment run.

## What changes if this works
The exception list is produced from the two registers rather than assembled by hand, so the clerk
starts the week with the dozen items that need a person instead of the ninety that do not. The two
hours go back to chasing the exceptions rather than finding them.
